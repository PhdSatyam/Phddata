Downloads

All files are gzip-compressed CSV, UTF-8. Cleaned versions: units normalised, cross-year checks applied, rows carry quality flags.

Financial statements

Money values are in ₹ crore; EPS is as printed. fin_tidy has one row per company, basis, fiscal year and field; the column flagged_scale_outlier is 1 for years that failed a plausibility check. In the wide files, qc_flags and xyear_notes list per-row checks and cross-year repairs.

BRSR tables

Columns: symbol, fy, section, indicator_type, pdf_page, table_no, row_no, c1…c15 (cells as printed), method, qc. The qc column marks rows that were split or merged, corrected from another year's report, or that disagree with it. See About & data quality.

The full extracted running text of the reports (about 360 MB) is not hosted here because of size limits.
