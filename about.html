About and data quality

What this is

Tables from the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) section of annual reports of NSE-listed companies, for reporting years 2022-23 to 2025-26. The source documents are the annual reports the companies filed publicly.

How it was made

Each report PDF was searched for its BRSR pages, and tables were extracted automatically and sorted into Section A, Section B and Principles 1–9. Cells are kept as printed, so units and number formats vary between companies.

Known limitations

  • Extraction is automatic. Rows can be split, merged, shifted or missing, especially for tables that span pages or use unusual layouts. Check anything important against the original report.
  • In some reports, especially those printed as two-page spreads or with side-by-side columns, text from neighbouring columns or pages is mixed into the same cells, so a table can be hard to read. The raw tables are best treated as a starting point for finding the right page, not as clean data.
  • Some tables could not be assigned to a section and are listed as "Unclassified".
  • A few reports are missing or could not be read in full (seven were not available, and some files were truncated at source).
  • Company names come from Section A where it could be read; otherwise only the NSE symbol is shown.
  • Some reports carry year labels such as 2022-2022 or 2024-2026 because of how the source files were named. They are kept as in the source.
  • Financial statement data is not on this site yet.
