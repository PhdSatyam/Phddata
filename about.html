About & data quality

This is a research dataset built automatically from public annual reports of NSE-listed companies. It is useful for exploration and for finding the right page of a report. It is not audited data. Read this page before relying on any number.

What is here

Companies1,270 with financials
BRSR reports4,176 company-years, 1,234 companies
All four BRSR cycles790 companies
Full history422 with all 4 BRSR cycles and every year FY2010-11 to FY2025-26

How it was made

Financial statements. The balance sheet, profit and loss and cash-flow pages of each report were located, read line by line and mapped to standard fields. Each statement's printed unit (rupees, thousand, lakh, million, crore) was detected and every amount converted to ₹ crore; earnings per share is kept as printed. Where an adjacent year's report shows the same figure as its prior-year column, it was used to fill gaps and to correct scale errors.

BRSR tables. Pages of the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report were found in each report and tables extracted and sorted into Section A, Section B and Principles 1–9. Cells are kept as printed, so units and formats vary by company. Text artefacts (ligatures, broken digits) were repaired, and cells that merged several rows were split where the pattern was clear.

Plausibility flags

Years are flagged when assets or revenue jump out of line with neighbouring years, or when profit, revenue and assets do not fit together. About 7% of company-years are flagged (more for banks, whose statements are often printed in thousands). Flagged years are hidden by default and shown in red when you ask to see them. Values that pass these checks can still be wrong.

BRSR cross-year check

Each report's figures were compared with the prior-year column of the next year's report: 114,760 comparable numeric cells, of which 90.6% agree. Disagreements are usually restatements or table misalignment and are marked in red in the BRSR view rather than overwritten. Empty cells were filled from a later report where the value could be confirmed on the original page. Principle 6 (environment) was checked in full against the PDFs; other sections were sampled.

Known limitations

  • Banks, older years: early-year bank statements are the least reliable. Several are flagged or missing. Use bank figures before FY2018 with particular care.
  • Repaired values: a small share of financial values were filled or replaced from the next year's report and cannot be verified from their own report. They are listed in the xyear_notes column of the downloads.
  • Fiscal years: a year is labelled by the fiscal year it ends in (FY2021-22 ends March 2022). Calendar-year reporters are shown under the year in which they end and noted on the company page.
  • Coverage: about 305 scanned reports could not be read (they need OCR) and 17 files are stubs. Companies that listed after 2011 have shorter histories. 432 reports had no BRSR section found.
  • Hindi and Marathi text in some BRSR tables comes out garbled because of legacy fonts.
  • BRSR tables can still contain split, merged, shifted or missing rows, and text from neighbouring columns. Rows with a coloured edge were corrected, disagree with another year, or have an unverified split.
  • Names: company names come from BRSR Section A where readable; otherwise only the NSE symbol is shown.
